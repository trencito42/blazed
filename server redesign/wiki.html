Beginner's
Protocol

Hardcore economy. We do not sell success; we leave it to your execution skills. Follow the technical onboarding sequence before deploying onto the server.

1. Spawn Initialization

Upon character generation, your entity is initialized at LSIA Airport or Central Station with minimal subsistence funds. Your first action is opening the telemetry dashboard via M or typing /stats.

2. Target UI Interaction

World interaction is native: press E near any ATM, Fleeca Bank, or 24/7 store. To interact with another player (inventory trade, inspection, actions), align yourself with them and hold G (or command /interact).

Zero Meta-Gaming Tolerance: The numeric session ID shown in player nametags is an administrative network address. Injecting this ID into In-Character dialogue is strictly prohibited and instantly penalized.

3. Mobile Terminal

Your smartphone is the operations center. Open it quickly by pressing P:

  • Execute encrypted bank transfers via the Fleeca Bank app.
  • Dispatch service requests for transportation (Taxi) or repairs (Mechanic).
  • Transmit emergency broadcasts directly to the 911 dispatch terminal (LSPD / EMS).

4. Level Progression & Payday

Every 60 minutes of active gameplay, the engine triggers the Payday cycle: occupational salary, account interest, and +1 Respect Point (RP). Character levels are purchased algorithmically via /buylevel once threshold points are met.

Technical Policy
& Operational Conduct

Unambiguous rules. High-caliber roleplay standards. Protocol violations trigger automated or administrative penalties.

1. Fundamental Terminology

RDM (Random Deathmatch): Neutralizing or harming another entity without validated In-Character motive and prior dialogue. Penalty: 90-180 min Jail / Temporary Ban.

VDM (Vehicle Deathmatch): Using a motor vehicle as a kinetic strike weapon to eliminate pedestrians. Penalty: 120 min Jail / 3-day Ban.

Meta-Gaming (MG): Importing Out-Of-Character intelligence (Discord, streams, nametag IDs) into live IC scenarios. Penalty: 60 min Mute / 60 min Jail.

Power-Gaming (PG): Executing physically impossible actions or forcing an outcome without offering the opposing party a reaction window. Penalty: Warning / 60 min Jail.

Combat Logging: Force-terminating the client (Alt+F4 / disconnect) during an engagement, arrest, or robbery. Penalty: 7-day Ban and asset wipe.

Zero Tolerance for Exploitation: Any item duplication attempts, unauthorized script injections, or geometry glitches result in an Immediate Permanent Ban across Hardware ID and Rockstar License.

Core Systems
& Architecture

XODO engineering kernel: native State Bag synchronization, calibrated 3D audio, and real-time NUI recovery.

1. Level Formula & Respect Points (RP)

On XODO RP, character level is strictly decoupled from civilian occupations. Jobs develop only Job Skill (Levels 1 to 5). Character level reflects seniority and consistency in the city and is purchased algorithmically using /buylevel with Respect Points accumulated at Payday.

2. Spatial 3D Voice Engine (Mumble)

Voice transmission is executed by holding N (Push-to-Talk). The HUD microphone pulses cyan neon during active voice streams. Tap Z to toggle coverage range:

  • WHISPER (1.5 meters): Confidential in-vehicle communication.
  • NORMAL (4.0 meters): Standard urban conversational perimeter.
  • SHOUT (10.0 meters): Tactical enforcement, orders, and long-range coordination.

3. Zero Player Dependencies & Recovery

If 0 medics (EMS) are online, solo bleedout duration automatically drops to 15 seconds. If 0 police units are active, armed robberies trigger a 3-star GTA V tactical police response. In case of unexpected NUI focus loss, /fixnui and /cursor instantly recover mouse input.

Economic Model
& Payday Cycle

Closed-circuit economy with zero artificial currency inflation. Every dollar carries physical mass and is recorded in SQL.

1. Fund Segregation: Cash vs. Bank

Physical Cash is carried in the pocket and vulnerable to underworld activities. Capital stored inside the Fleeca Central Core is cyber-secured and generates compounded interest on each Payday cycle.

2. Hourly Payday Execution

Every 60 active minutes, the server completes fiscal ledger reconciliation:

  • Activity Salary: Direct deposit based on civilian employment or faction grade.
  • Respect Point: +1 RP credited toward your next /buylevel milestone.
  • Banking Yield: +0.1% to 0.25% yield calculated against active Fleeca deposits.
  • Rent Deduction: Automated lease deduction for players registered as property tenants.

Dealership, Garages
& Vehicle Telemetry

Rebuilt handling pipeline, coordinate persistence via /park, encrypted locking, and ECU chip tuning.

1. Coordinate Persistence (/park)

While piloting your personal vehicle, run /park. The engine freezes your exact transformation matrix (X, Y, Z coordinates and heading). Upon your next spawn, the vehicle materializes precisely where it was left.

2. Fleet Operations Matrix

Use the following key mappings for vehicle operations:

  • U or /lock: Toggle door lockdown (restricted to key holder).
  • 2: Manual engine ignition toggle.
  • K: Engage safety harness to prevent cabin ejection during high-speed impacts.
  • H: Cycle headlight telemetry (Off → Low Beam → High Beam).
  • /v: Personal garage management and GPS tracking.

3. LS Customs & ECU Chip Remapping

Visit LS Customs facilities for rapid diagnostics ($250), aero packages, paint, and ECU Chip Remapping: custom torque curves, rev limiters, and instant throttle response curves.

Grid Inventory
& Crafting Engine

5x8 binary grid layout, item mass weighting, atomic Trade transaction protocol, and industrial fabrication.

1. Storage Matrix (Key I)

Press I or type /inventory to launch the 40-slot grid (5x8). Each item carries mass in kilograms. Click on any asset to Use or Drop (dropped bags persist in the world for 5 minutes).

2. Atomic Trade Protocol

To securely exchange commodities with a player within 3.5 meters:

  1. Press G → Trade Request (or command /trade [id]).
  2. Target player accepts by holding Y or rejects via N.
  3. In the dual console, allocate items and currency values.
  4. Both parties lock. Slots are hardware-locked against duplication until atomic ledger commit completes.

3. Industrial Crafting

At certified workbenches, combine raw resources (Iron, Copper, Electronic Components, Rubber) to manufacture trauma kits, ballistic vests, lockpicks, and replacement automotive parts.
